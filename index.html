Clip-Uploader

Clip-Uploader is a private publishing tool used by the owner of the Glitch Perfect YouTube channel. It helps prepare short clips and upload them to that one channel after the owner has reviewed them.

What it does

  • Uploads approved videos to the Glitch Perfect channel through the YouTube Data API.
  • Reads basic channel and video information so the owner can confirm uploads and review performance.

Who can use it

Clip-Uploader is not a public service and has no sign-up. Only the channel owner connects a Google account to it.

▶ Uses YouTube API Services

Clip-Uploader publishes to the Glitch Perfect channel on YouTube. Read the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service to see how data is handled.
