Clip-Uploader

Privacy Policy

Last updated November 10, 2026

Clip-Uploader is a private tool operated by the owner of the Glitch Perfect YouTube channel. Only the channel owner connects a Google account to it.

YouTube API Services

Clip-Uploader uses YouTube API Services. By using it, you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Information accessed

  • Permission to upload videos to the connected YouTube channel.
  • Read-only channel and video information, such as the channel name, video IDs, titles, upload status and performance statistics.
  • A Google authorization token that lets the tool act on the connected channel.

How information is used

This information is used only to upload approved videos to the connected channel, confirm those uploads, and help the channel owner review performance.

Storage

The authorization token and related files are stored on the channel owner's computer. Clip-Uploader does not run a public database of user information.

Sharing

Clip-Uploader does not sell this information or use it for advertising. Video transcripts and channel performance summaries may be processed by an AI service provider to help the channel owner choose clips and review results. Information may also be disclosed if required by law.

Cookies and tracking

Clip-Uploader's website does not set cookies or use analytics. The tool does not place cookies or tracking technology on the devices of YouTube viewers.

Revoking access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google security settings page. To request deletion of stored information, use the contact link below.

Changes

Updates to this policy will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions or requests can be sent through the project's GitHub Issues page.
